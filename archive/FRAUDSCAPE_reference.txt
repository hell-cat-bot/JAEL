Why the normal graph solution is becoming generic The original TVS
problem asks E to use collective intelligence to find hidden
relationships among: loan applications, devices, dealer networks, bank
accounts, mobile numbers, locations, guarantors and payment behaviour,
and predict emerging fraud ecosystems before fraud occurs. The obvious
solution is: Entities → Graph → GNN → Fraud Score → Ring Detection That
is already well represented in the research literature. For example, the
AAAI-21 consumer-loan paper explicitly models applicants, sellers,
intermediaries and other users as a multi-relational graph and uses a
role-aware GNN to detect collusive loan fraud. (AAAI Publications) More
recent research goes even further: Dynamic heterogeneous GNNs model
evolving fraud relationships. (PubMed) Contrastive learning is being
used because fraud labels arrive late or are scarce. (PubMed)
