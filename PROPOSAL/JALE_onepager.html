1 · The fraud war TVS fights is a lending war PUBLIC

Fraud value is not in payments — it is in advances. Card/internet fraud is a rounding error against the loan book.

Channel (RBI AR 2025-26)FY26 valueShare
Advances (loans)₹40,774 cr85%

And each case is getting bigger. Average fraud value per case: FY24 ₹2.17 cr → FY25 ₹3.83 cr → FY26 ₹4.72 cr = +118% severity per case in two years. Rings are consolidating, not multiplying — which is exactly what a graph, not a per-application score, is built to catch.

3 · Why you can trust the number COLAB + SIM

Three controls, all passing:

  • Shuffled labels → model drops to chance (no memorisation).
  • Ring-disjoint CV → test rings never leak into training.
  • Nested CV → hyperparameters chosen inside inner folds only.

The leakage gap — inflation from a naive random split: synthetic TVS-like graph +10.0% AUCPR; real graph (YelpChi) +2.2%. We report the harder number.

And we tell you where we fail: hold out one typology and AUCPR drops to 0.27. We ship that as a built-in novelty detector — a flag for unseen structures — rather than claiming we understand all rings.

5 · Which connection actually carries the risk? COLAB

The dealer is a conduit, not the cause. Blocking the dealer changes almost nothing about ring detection; blocking the bank-account link collapses it.

Design consequence: verify the cheap link (account, device); never blanket-block the dealer. This protects genuine rural borrowers.

6 · Ring-vaccination: the cheapest link that breaks the ring SIM

Score the cluster, not the person. A ring-level queue with an evidence case-file per cluster, showing the dominant axis: device / account / guarantor / burst.

Actions are firebreaks — minimal-friction, budget-aware:

  • Step-up: verify one disbursement account
  • Verify the guarantor pool
  • Hold disbursement to one account
  • Dealer audit only when implicated by the ablation

Every action maps to a measured signal drop: account −0.22 · device −0.21 · dealer −0.10 (least signal). Analyst budget → recovery: reviewing the top-k risk clusters recovers ~22% of injected ring applications at k=1 rising to ~64% at k=20.

Repo L4 evaluation: all 12 rings flagged at ≥0.5, 83% of fraud apps covered (doubts.md D14).
