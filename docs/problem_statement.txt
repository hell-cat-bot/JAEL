Problem Statement
You can select any one of the process areas below, bringing in disruption to the existing status quo:
 a
Predictive AI/ML Early-Warning Signals for Collections
Using machine learning models on a Loan Management System (LMS) to track
state/region-wise macro-economic factors, localized weather shifts, past EMI payment
timelines to predict default risk.
 b
Decoding Machine-Generated Trust
Develop an AI-powered solution capable of distinguishing humans from machines by
detecting synthetic identities, cloned voices, deepfake customers, AI-generated voice calls,
automated loan applications, and AI-powered impersonation attacks, enabling financial
institutions to prevent next-generation fraud before it occurs.
 c
AI-Powered Smart Lending Decision Hub
Development of an integrated AI-driven lending platform that combines AI-based lending
for agriculture based customers using satellite data, weather, crop and geo-location
insights, AI credit scoring, fraud detection, loan recommendation engine, default prediction,
GenAI loan assistant, real-time risk dashboards, and early-warning mechanisms for
defaulters.
 d
Alternative Data Credit Engine for the Invisible Customer
Develop an AI-powered alternative credit scoring engine leveraging GST data, UPI
transaction trends, telecom recharge patterns, utility bill payments, e-commerce activity,
and mobility and vehicle usage patterns to generate explainable risk scores for first-time
borrowers, gig workers, small merchants, and informal sector workers.
 e
Swarm Intelligence Lending Network
Create an AI-driven collective intelligence platform that continuously learns and identifies
hidden relationships across loan applications, device fingerprints, dealer networks, bank
accounts, mobile numbers, locations, guarantors, and payment behaviours to predict
emerging fraud ecosystems before fraud occurs.

f
AI-Powered Financial Resilience Score
Develop an AI-based scoring framework that evaluates a customer's financial stability and
repayment capability under adverse economic conditions using transaction behaviour,
income stability, employment history, EMI repayment patterns, alternative financial
indicators, and economic shock simulations to generate risk classifications, explainable
scorecards, early-warning indicators, and personalized lending recommendations.
 g
Intelligent Merchant & Product Risk Engine
The rapid growth of e-commerce financing introduces new risks for lenders. Product return
fraud, merchant service issues, fake transactions, and sudden shifts in consumer buying
behavior is common and can significantly impact portfolio performance and profitability.
Identifying these risks early is critical for sustainable lending.
Develop an AI-powered merchant and product risk monitoring platform that continuously
analyses product performance, return and cancellation rates, fulfilment quality, customer
feedback, regional anomalies, seasonal trends, transaction behaviour, and suspicious
activities to provide real-time risk dashboards, merchant risk scoring, fraud detection, risk
alerts, and actionable recommendations for proactive risk mitigation.
